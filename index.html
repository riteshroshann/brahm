About the fest
73 Years of
Pure Love & Light
Brahm 2026 is the Founders’ Day Fest of Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Delhi NCR Campus — five days built around the birth anniversary of Sri Mata Amritanandamayi Devi, celebrated across every Amrita campus.
The week opens with seva — free health check-up camps in the villages nearby — and moves through nine inter-college cultural competitions, a marathon, an auto expo, carnival and DJ nights, three days of in-house sport, and closes with the Founders’ Day function in the Main Auditorium on 23 September.
Celebrating health, talent, unity and joy.
