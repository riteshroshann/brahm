About the fest

73 years of
pure love & light

Brahm 2026 is the Founders’ Day Fest of Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Delhi NCR Campus — five days built around the birth anniversary of Sri Mata Amritanandamayi Devi, celebrated across every Amrita campus.

The week opens with seva — free health check-up camps in the villages nearby — and moves through nine inter-college cultural competitions, a marathon, an auto expo, carnival and DJ nights, three days of in-house sport, and closes with the Founders’ Day function in the Amriteshwari Auditorium on 23rd September.
